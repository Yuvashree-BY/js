About One Piece

One Piece is one of the most popular anime and manga series created by Eiichiro Oda. It follows the adventures of Monkey D. Luffy, a young pirate whose body gained the properties of rubber after eating a Devil Fruit.

His dream is to become the King of the Pirates by finding the legendary treasure known as "One Piece".
